# Express Toll Pass

Track, audit, and manage your highway transponder and toll expenses effortlessly.

---

## Description
Express Toll Pass is an enterprise-ready, client-side financial tracking dashboard purpose-built for modern logistics, daily commuters, and commercial fleet operators who utilize electronic toll transponders. Managing highway expenditures across multiple vehicles, accounts, and transit authorities often leads to accounting fragmentation and missed tax deductions. This application solves that operational bottleneck by providing a highly structured, localized environment to log individual toll costs, filter operational data, and instantly compile digital receipts. Designed with a clean, responsive user interface, it bridges the gap between raw transit logs and clean financial records. Users can input exact toll plazas, tag specific corporate or personal vehicles, catalog fees, and export cleanly formatted summaries perfect for reimbursement submissions or tax season documentation. By streamlining data entry and automating balance calculations, Express Toll Pass eliminates the guesswork from commuter expense tracking, ensuring that every bridge crossing, express lane fee, and highway charge is accounted for down to the last penny.

---

## Features
* **Real-time Expense Logging:** Quickly input toll amounts, plaza locations, transponder types, and specific dates.
* **Instant Digital Receipts:** Dynamically generates printable, itemized financial receipts for accounting and employer reimbursements.
* **Live Balance Analytics:** Automatically sums up total toll expenditures on the fly.
* **Responsive Architecture:** Fully optimized for seamless mobile tracking on the road or desktop audits at the office.
